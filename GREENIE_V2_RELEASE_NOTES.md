# Greenie Vietnam B2B Website v2 — release notes

## Scope
- New Heritage & Trust homepage with existing product URLs preserved.
- Product priority: cashew kernels and artisan cane sugar; Shan Tuyet tea remains linked.
- Replaced unverifiable counters and implied certification badges with conditional sourcing and quality copy.
- Added export solutions, commercial terms marked as indicative, and OEM overview.
- Expanded RFQ form to include company, contact, destination, quantity, terms and requirements.
- Fixed mobile navigation semantics and accessible menu state.
- Email form creates a draft in the visitor's mail application. It **does not deliver, store, or acknowledge receipt of RFQs**.

## Business facts requiring verification before production
- MOQ from 1 MT and estimated lead time 7–15 days **only for eligible products**.
- Port of loading, FOB/CIF choices and payment terms by SKU and destination.
- Product-grade availability and any manufacturer-specific certificates.
- Current contact email and WhatsApp ownership.
- Source of product photos and any rights to use them.
- Whether Shan Tuyet tea remains an active trade offering.
- Any manufacturer use of egg white for artisan cane sugar and related allergen/halal implications.

## Important follow-on work
1. Audit **individual product pages**, which may still contain unsupported certification, pesticide, traceability or organic claims.
2. Replace mailto draft workflow with a secure backend provider only after selecting a recipient and data/privacy policy.
3. Validate on desktop/mobile browsers, real mail-app setups and GitHub Pages/hosting staging.
4. Run link, contrast, performance and accessibility checks.
5. Obtain approval before merging to main or deploying production.

## Technical notes
Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript project. No dependency or framework migration.
CSS and JS changed only for the shared design system and homepage enhancement.
