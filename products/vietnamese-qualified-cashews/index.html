The Single-Origin Cashew

Red Basalt Soil of Binh Phuoc

Terroir Story: Volcanic Basalt Soil

The unique basalt soil of Binh Phuoc yields cashews with superior density, buttery richness, and a sweet aftertaste unmatched by imported raw nuts.

Large bamboo trays of cashews are sun-dried naturally under the intense Southeast Vietnamese sun, preserving flavor integrity.

Why Partner With Greenie's Binh Phuoc Cashews?

Unlike mixed-origin cashews frequently found on the commodity market, Greenie Vietnam exclusively sources from Binh Phuoc province. This region is globally recognized for producing kernels with a denser structure, a slightly sweeter flavor profile, and superior roasting characteristics. This single-origin commitment ensures complete traceability from the farm gate to your warehouse, guaranteeing consistent quality batch after batch.

We understand that our B2B partners require absolute reliability in food safety. Our partner facilities employ optical sorting, metal detection, and strict moisture control protocols. Every container is accompanied by comprehensive documentation including Certificates of Analysis (COA), Phytosanitary Certificates, and independent Aflatoxin testing results, ensuring seamless customs clearance and immediate integration into your manufacturing or retail packaging processes.

Frequently Asked Questions (B2B)

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

Our standard MOQ is 1 FCL (Full Container Load) - approximately 15-16 metric tons for a 20ft container. For smaller trial orders or LCL shipments, please contact our wholesale team.

What are the standard payment terms?

We typically operate on T/T (Telegraphic Transfer) with a 30% advance deposit and 70% against the copy of B/L, or 100% Irrevocable L/C at sight for established partners.

Do you offer private label packaging?

Yes, in addition to standard 50lbs bulk cartons, we offer OEM/Private Label solutions including retail-ready vacuum pouches and nitrogen-flushed tins.

How long is the typical lead time?

From order confirmation to FOB Ho Chi Minh City port, the standard lead time is 14 to 21 days depending on packaging requirements and volume.
