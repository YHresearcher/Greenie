Artisan Unrefined Cane Sugar

Quang Ngai Craft Villages

Cultural Heritage: The Traditional Sugar House

Inside the historic sugar huts of Quang Ngai, artisans stand over boiling molasses vats, precisely timing the strike of the cane juice.

Using natural egg whites to clarify impurities, followed by rapid whipping to create a porous, airy structure resembling a leaf of a lung (hence 'Duong Phoi').

B2B Position: Premium Natural Sweetening Solution

A clean alternative to refined sugar, aligned with Europe's growing macrobiotic food trend. Clarified strictly through mechanical means and egg whites, free of chemical bleaching agents, retaining natural minerals.

B2B Targets Specialty cafe chains, bubble tea networks, bird's nest producers, and organic food processors
Key Characteristics Mild, non-cloying sweetness with zero chemical industrial bleaching
EU Market Compliance Pesticide-free, preservative-free, retaining iron and calcium from cane juice
Packaging Options Glass jars, Kraft pouches, or wholesale bulk boxes
