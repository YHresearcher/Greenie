Cultural Heritage: The Traditional Sugar House

Inside the historic sugar huts of Quang Ngai, artisans stand over boiling molasses vats, precisely timing the strike of the cane juice.

Using natural egg whites to clarify impurities, followed by rapid whipping to create a porous, airy structure resembling a leaf of a lung (hence 'Duong Phoi').

Frequently Asked Questions (B2B)

What makes this different from brown sugar?

Commercial brown sugar is often just refined white sugar sprayed with molasses. Our artisan cane sugar is entirely unrefined, crystallized directly from the cane juice using mechanical and thermal processes, retaining its original molasses content.

How is it packaged for bulk wholesale?

We offer 25kg and 50kg moisture-resistant, food-grade Kraft bags for bulk industrial use, as well as customizable glass jars or pouches for retail-ready distribution (OEM).

Is this suitable for vegan products?

Historically, the clarification process utilized egg whites, making it vegetarian but not strictly vegan. However, we offer specific batches clarified using modern mechanical filtration (bone-char free) suitable for vegan certification.

Can I request a sample before committing to an MOQ?

Absolutely. We provide 500g samples dispatched via DHL or FedEx so your R&D and QA teams can evaluate the taste profile, solubility, and quality directly.
